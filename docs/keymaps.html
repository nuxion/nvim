Conflicts & gotchas

  • ⎵f vs ⎵ff — conform waits for timeoutlen (default 1000 ms) before firing ⎵f, because ⎵ff is a longer mapping. Pause deliberately, or drop one of them.
  • ⎵t vs ⎵tt — same story inside a Fugitive buffer: ⎵t (push -u origin) delays until the timeout because Trouble owns ⎵tt.
  • ⎵p vs ⎵pf/pg/… — inside a Fugitive buffer, ⎵p (push) shadows the Telescope ⎵p… prefix until the timeout elapses. Outside Fugitive, the Telescope maps work normally.
  • Ctrl+p means git-files in normal mode, but previous completion item in insert mode. No real conflict — different modes.
  • Ctrl+e is Harpoon's menu in normal mode and cmp's abort in insert mode. Also different modes.
  • Format on save can fight ⎵ff — vim.lsp.buf.format and conform may disagree for filetypes where both are available (Python: ruff via both paths).
